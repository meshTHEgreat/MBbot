02 · The field

What market was this?

Short-dated listed options, bought and closed during the same US trading session.

An option is a time-limited contract linked to another asset, the underlying. A call benefits from an upward move; a put benefits from a downward move. This program bought calls and puts on SPY, QQQ, AAPL, NVDA, and TSLA. Its core mandate used 1 to 4 days to expiration and closed intraday, with no overnight carry.S1GS

Buying immediately means paying the ask. Selling immediately means accepting the bid. The distance between them is the spread. The midpoint between bid and ask is useful as a diagnostic, but it is not the conservative executable ledger. Reference commissions were $0.65 per contract per side; stress used $1.30 per side.S1

The central economic question became: can the realized move during the hold exceed what the option market already priced, plus this unavoidable round-trip toll? Program 3 named that difference NETGAP.N2

INDICATOR / STRATEGY

A reading is not a trading system.

MACD or RSI can describe a chart. A strategy also needs a causal hypothesis, regime, trigger, contract rule, execution, risk, and exit.

TRIGGER / FILTER

One creates an order; one only vetoes it.

A trigger authorizes entry. A spread cap can reject that entry. A filter can improve the average by removing bad trades without creating predictive direction.

ENTRY / EXECUTION / EXIT

Three separate clocks.

Entry is the commitment event. Execution is the modeled or obtained fill. Exit is the rule that closes the position. Changing one changes economics even if the signal is untouched.

RELATIVE / ABSOLUTE

Better than a control can still lose money.

Gen5's eligible class beat its matched control by +$2.9629, while its MT cell estimate was only +$0.1282 and the later portfolio was negative. Relative improvement is not absolute positive expectancy.

The complete G6 portfolio policy

This was the first complete cash-to-orders-to-positions ledger. Earlier campaigns had some trade caps and cooldowns, but not most of this frozen package operating together. That is why their per-trade means should not be read as portfolio returns.GS

Capital base$100,000 initial cash

Cash, orders, positions, daily P&L, and drawdown were updated chronologically.

Order size1 contract

Exactly one contract per accepted option order.

Symbol concurrency1 open contract

At most one concurrent contract per symbol; later same-symbol entries were dropped.

Session frequency3 trades maximum

At most three accepted trades per symbol per session.

Cross-symbol exposureSPY + QQQ count as one

Same-direction SPY and QQQ exposure could not be simultaneous. Opposite directions were allowed subject to all other caps.

Cooldown30 minutes after exit

The same rule followed wins, losses, or zero. A direction exiting in either ETF blocked that direction in both for 30 minutes.

Position premium$1,000 maximum ask

An order above the maximum entry ask premium was declined.

Open premium risk$2,000 aggregate

Total premium risk across open positions could not exceed the cap.

Daily stress risk$2,000 cap

Admission included nonnegative realized stress losses, open-position maximum losses, and the proposed position. New entries stopped at the cap.

Capital reservationPremium + both fees

At entry, the ledger reserved ask × 100, entry commission, and the stress-level exit commission. An order was dropped when available cash could not fund the reservation.

Event orderingExits before entries

At equal timestamps, exits ran first; entries then used registered score and candidate-ID priority.

ExitExactly +60 minutes

No profit target, stop, early exit, extension, or outcome-based rewrite.

How to read earlier numbers

Campaign 3's +$3.3436 was a midpoint average per trade. Gen5's +$2.9629 was a matched relative difference. G6's +$9.5226 was an unconstrained opportunity average. None of those is the same object as the -$2,269.90 chronological portfolio result.

05 · Combination map

What was combined with what

The project did test stacks and interactions, but not every imaginable pairing.

Campaign 1

Indicator plus overlays

Option-premium MACD was paired with alternate bar sizes, MACD tuples, RSI, SMI, momentum, premium velocity, relative volume, spread and premium caps, DTE exclusion, Delta/Gamma/Theta filters, targets, stops, and exit changes. Most were gates on the same trigger.

Campaign 2

Single frozen events, then one formal stack

The screen tested 48 event definitions across four axes. The formal U08 family combined a fast/slow MA gap with aligned 15-minute return, then removed each component in two preregistered ablations.

Campaign 3

Opening range + time + liquidity

L10 combined the first 30-minute range break, a 09:45 onward entry time, and relative spread at most 1.20%. Ablations removed tight liquidity and removed the breakout buffer.

Gen5

Time × liquidity factorial

The frozen 2 × 2 design estimated later/original, later/tight, morning/original, and morning/tight cells, their main effects, and the difference-in-differences interaction.

Gen5B

Forecast over eligibility filter

Eleven causal one-minute feature groups fed four model families inside the replicated morning-tight class. Coverage levels of 5%, 10%, 20%, 40%, and 100% were frozen.

G6

Trailing sign + filter + portfolio

The registered aligned five-minute return sign ranked contracts inside the eligibility filter. The exact selected rows then met the complete G6 capital and concurrency policy.

Program 3

Regime × phase × DTE × group

Confirmatory states pooled symbols into ETFs or single names, pooled DTE into 1 to 2 or 3 to 4, required tight/liquid NTM eligibility, and crossed low/high volatility with morning or midday phase. The larger grid stayed exploratory and non-decisional.

Open, not a finding

Was a winning-trade signature ever paired with a losing-trade signature?

The listed artifacts do not document that named pairing as a frozen test, and they do not supply a canonical definition of either signature. This page therefore records it as an owner-supplied open question, not as evidence that the combination would help.

06 · Final mechanism

N1 asked the right question. N2 sharpened it.

The final answer depends on preserving that correction rather than repeating the first consultation's broader claim.

N1 · structural thesis
“Highest-value single study; may end or redirect the program.”

N1 argued that buyers pay variance-risk premium plus spread, that raw $1 to $3 effects were practically unconfirmable, and that the class should be tested formally before another signal was written.N1

N2 · controlling correction
“The decisive economic point survives regardless”

N2 said the premium may be concentrated overnight. It re-stated the intraday null as E[RM - IM - c] ≤ 0, made 0DTE a filtered measurement benchmark, and made BK's intraday-to-expiry split load-bearing.N2

1 · Gross movement

Realized did not clear implied.

Gross GAP was negative in every replication ETF cell and every 2025 to 2026 ETF cell. Some underpowered single-name gross cells were positive, but their NETGAP remained negative.

2 · Round-trip toll

Cost made the gate stricter.

The frozen minimum economic effect was 1.32 in underlying-move units. Every observed primary NETGAP was below zero.

3 · Horizon split

Intraday attenuated the premium.

Median BK per premium was negative intraday in 9 of 9 slices and less negative than to expiry in 8 of 9. Attenuation was real; a positive median intraday premium was not found.

Why the project stopped here

The frozen evaluator had no surviving confirmatory state, the exact trailing-sign lineage was already closed on portfolio and power grounds, and every exploratory or seller-side result was barred from changing the buyer decision. Continuing inside the same long-premium class would have required an unregistered mutation.

09 · Evidence hygiene

Disagreements kept visible

Some numbers only appear to conflict because they answer different questions. Two document-level integrity issues remain real.

Superseded thesis

N1's broad structural story versus N2's intraday null

N1 emphasized variance-risk premium plus spread. N2 said that premium may be concentrated overnight and replaced the decision null with intraday E[RM - IM - c] ≤ 0. N2 explicitly controls where they differ; Program 3 used the re-stated null.

Temporal, not conflict

The Campaign 3 page stopped after Stage 3 without the later incrementality and execution work. Campaign 4B then completed those gates and emitted retired_no_incremental_trigger_edge. Both statements are accurate at their own freeze points.

Design boundary

Five of 250 pipeline resamples passed, yet G6 did not treat that as closure.

Campaign 4B reported add-one p = 0.023904 for W5. G6 later recorded that 250 draws were too few for a stable decision boundary and required at least 10,000 for future confirmatory pipeline-null work. The small-run number remains published, but its sufficiency was downgraded.

Integrity limitation

Gen5 development used five-minute data where the preregistration required one minute.

The A2 development check therefore carried partial integrity. The independent A2 replication used the literal one-minute contract. The final replication claim rests on the latter, not on silently relabeling the development run.

Unresolved document conflict

Gen5B records B2 and C2 contamination; the G6 plan text calls the gate valid.

The Gen5B audit records 34 contaminated B2 rows and 36 contaminated C2 rows, while C3 was valid. A later G6 plan sentence says the existing validation gate was already met. Terminology correction

“Old-but-clean” does not mean causally clean.

Program 2's later clarification narrows “clean” to freedom from this project's selection history. It does not rule out regime drift, other researchers' discovery, provider artifacts, or ordinary confounding.

Different bars

The oracle atlas is 95.228628% alive in Phase A and 86.68% robust in Phase B.

Those percentages use different hurdles and denominators. Phase A's 2,395 alive rows are 95.228628% of 2,515 populated configurations, or 88.703704% of all 2,700. Phase B applies a stronger robust-alive definition. They should not be averaged or used interchangeably.

Different denominators

The final artifact distinguishes 30 formal outcomes from the locked baseline, diagnostics, and parity control. “Thirty configs” is therefore safe only when it means the formal-outcome family, not every configuration or control present in the combined ledger.
