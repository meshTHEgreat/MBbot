      How path evidence and replay outcomes are measured

      MAE is the worst executable-bid loss observed from entry through the recorded exit. An active stop can censor that original path. Replay outcomes are different: every threshold independently recomputes positions, exits, and later re-entries using the first eligible bid at the dataset's quote resolution.
